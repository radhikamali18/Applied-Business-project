# Business Analytics of Branded Electronics Sales

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes a **Branded Electronics Sales** dataset using Business Analytics and statistical techniques. The main aim is to understand sales performance, customer behaviour, marketing performance and future business trends using data.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze branded electronics sales data.
* Understand revenue and sales patterns.
* Study customer behaviour and conversion.
* Analyze the relationship between marketing spend and revenue.
* Perform statistical analysis and forecasting.
* Support better business decision-making using data.

## 🏢 Business Domain

**Branded Electronics Retail**

The dataset contains sales information for brands such as Apple, Samsung, Sony, OnePlus, Dell and HP across different cities in India.

## 📊 Dataset

* **Records:** 180
* **Columns:** 17
* **Time Period:** January 2025 – December 2025
* **Data Format:** CSV
